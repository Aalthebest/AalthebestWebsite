The Aberturret is a London Dry Gin made at The Glenturret in Crieff, Scotland's oldest working distillery. It is the distillery's first gin, released in 2024 alongside the opening of Aberturret House on the distillery estate.

## About the product

- **Style:** London Dry Gin
- **Alcohol:** 45% vol.
- **Volume:** 70 cl
- **Botanicals:** 14, including juniper, lemon, lime, grapefruit, chamomile, pine, pink peppercorn and sumac
- **Distinctive feature:** The Glenturret's single malt new make spirit is part of the recipe

The emerald green bottle was designed by Lalique and is based on the distillery's whisky bottle.
