Look out for The Glenturret at Oslo Whisky Festival in Norway this November (Friday 6th & Saturday 7th).

Assistant Distillery Manager, Matthew Farmer, will be presenting five of our whiskies alongside a seminar where he will share the craftsmanship and stories behind Scotland's oldest working distillery.

Our distribution in Norway is through importer Aal the Best AS, founded by Mr. Henning-A. Frantzen.

Discover more at [hubs.la/Q04zKqfP0](https://hubs.la/Q04zKqfP0)

- **Friday 6 November:** 17:00–21:00
- **Saturday 7 November:** 13:00–20:00
- **Venue:** Thon Conference Universitetsgate, Universitetsgata 26, Oslo

Tickets and programme are available at [owf.no](https://owf.no).
