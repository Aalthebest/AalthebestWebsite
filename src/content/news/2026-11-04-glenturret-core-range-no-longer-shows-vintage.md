From 2026, The Glenturret will no longer display the vintage of its core range (Triple Wood, 12 Years Old and 15 Years Old) on the label and box.

This means that the 12 Years Old and 15 Years Old released in Norway on 4 November are from the 2026 core range.

Please enjoy.
